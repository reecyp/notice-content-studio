## Slides

### Slide 1

I'm 27. If you're in
your 20's, do this:

### Slide 2

1. Fall in love with exercise. Make it a part of
your weekly rhythm. Training doesn't just
build your body, it literally rewires your brain
and sets the stage for every other positive
change in your life.
2. Cut out the enablers. If someone's always
encouraging your bad habits, they're not a
friend. Surround yourself with people who
want the best for you, not the worst.

### Slide 3

3. Shrink your circle, but upgrade it's quality.
Spend time with people who are driven and
building something. Your network truly
becomes your net worth.
4. Replace soda and juice With water. Cutting
the excess sugar and staying hydrated will
improve your energy, skin, and long-term
health.

### Slide 4

5. Invest in self-development. Read the books.
You can try our books. Find the link in our bio.
Learn from those who've already walked the
path. Your brain is your greatest asset, feed it.
6. Master your mindset. Thoughts become
beliefs. Beliefs become behaviors. If you don't
learn to tame your mind, it will tame you.
7. Turn doubt into fuel. When people
underestimate you, don't sulk, smile. Every
doubt thrown at you is just another log for
your fire.

### Slide 5

8. Use modeling as a shortcut. If someone else
has done it, you can too. Don't overcomplicate
it, we're all human and capable of more than
we think.
9. Aim for the top. Most people are just trying
to "get by." The higher you set your standard,
the less competition you'll face.
10. Visualize your future. Everything you
achieve starts as a picture in your mind.
If you can't see it, you'll never create it.

### Slide 6

11. Stop living for paternal approval. Ask
yourself: if your parent were gone, what
would you pursue? That's the path that's true
to you.
12. Quit complaining. Stop being offended.
Refuse to play the victim. Energy spent
whining is energy you could use winning.
13. Adopt an abundance mindset. Scarcity
keeps you small. There's more than enough
to go around, focus on creating, not clinging.

### Slide 7

It's k
about thinking differently. Operating
differently. Building the mental frameworks
that turn discipline into default behavior.
Most people will keep winging it, hoping
consistency just "clicks' one day.
You're not most people.
