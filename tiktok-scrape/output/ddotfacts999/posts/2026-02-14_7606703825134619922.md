### Slide 1

The 80/20 rule applied to life:
(keep only these 6 things)

### Slide 2

1. Morning routine
(first 60 minutes = entire day)
Here's what helps:
• Wake up at 5:30 AM
• Drink water immediately (you're dehydrated)
• Move for 20 minutes (walk, stretch, or train)
• Avoid your phone until 7 AM
Master your morning or it masters you. It's that
straightforward.

### Slide 3

2. Health
(your foundation for everything)
Exercise 4 days. Rest for 3. Simple as that.
80% of fitness is consistency, not perfect
routines.
Sleep 8 hours gives more benefits than any
supplement.
Protein + vegetables + cover most dietary needs.
Skip complexity. Master the basics.

### Slide 4

3. Key relationships (5 people, not 500)
A large network isn't required.
You need:
• One mentor who's ahead
• Two peers at your level for motivation
• Two individuals you're helping
This is your circle. Everything else is social
media noise.

### Slide 5

4. Income-generating skills
(go inch-wide, mile-deep)
Most learn many skills:
marketing, coding, design, sales,
writing, speaking...
Top performers choose one skill.
Refine it for 2-3 years.
Reach the top 5% in it.
Money comes to you after that.

### Slide 6

5. Deep work (3 hours beats 10 hours)
Here's the truth:
2-3 hours of deep, focused work beats a full
day of "busy work."
Turn off your phone in 90-minute blocks.
Work on one project until it's done.
The person who finishes one task beats the
person who starts ten.

### Slide 7

6. Mental clarity
(your headspace - your results)
Every Sunday, do this:
• List 3 priorities for the week
• Remove all other tasks
• Most stress comes from overcommitting
Decline good opportunities.
Say yes only to the exceptional ones.
Clarity isn't about knowing more - it's
about simplifying what you do.

### Slide 8

It's about thinking differently. Operating
differently. Building the mental frameworks
that turn discipline into default behavior.
