How To Learn Extremely

### Slide 2

Teach Everything You
Learn.
fastest way to identify
gaps in your
understanding is trying to
explain an idea to
someone else. If you
struggle to explain it
simply, you probably don't
understand it.

### Slide 3

Learn With A Purpose.
Your brain remembers
information that feels
useful. Before learning
something new, ask
yourself exactly how
you'll use it. Information
without application is
usually forgotten quickly.

### Slide 4

Study Mistakes
Aggressively
Success often hides
weaknesses. Mistakes
expose them. Every error
contains information
about what needs to
improve, making failure
one of the fastest
teachers available.

### Slide 5

Use The "Learn → Apply
Apply •
→ Reflect" Cycle.
Don't spend weeks
consuming information
before taking action.
Learn something, apply it
immediately, then reflect
on what worked and what
didn't. This creates much
faster learning loops.
